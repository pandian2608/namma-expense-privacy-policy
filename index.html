Privacy Policy

Namma Expense

Effective date: 12 November 2026

Publisher: Tamizhstacks

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Namma Expense (“the App”), provided by Tamizhstacks (“we”, “us” or “our”), handles information when you use the App. Namma Expense is a personal finance and expense-tracking tool designed to store your records on your device.

2. Information you enter and store

The App allows you to enter and maintain information such as:

  • Income and expense transactions, including amounts, dates, categories, payment methods and notes;
  • Budgets, savings goals and contributions;
  • Shared-expense groups, participants, shares and settlement records;
  • Bill reminders and bill-payment history;
  • App activity, streaks and achievement information;
  • Your display name and app preferences, such as currency display, date and number formats, and theme;
  • App-lock settings, including a salted one-way hash of your PIN and recovery code.

This information is stored locally in the App's database and app storage on your device. The App does not require you to create an account or sign in.

3. Information collection and transmission

Based on the current application source code reviewed for this policy, Namma Expense does not include an app-operated account service, cloud synchronisation service, advertising SDK, analytics SDK or crash-reporting SDK. The reviewed app code does not send the financial records you enter to Tamizhstacks or to an app-operated remote server.

The App's core finance features are designed to work on your device. This statement describes the reviewed build; it should be checked again if future versions add online services, analytics, advertising, crash reporting or other third-party SDKs.

4. How information is used

Information you enter is used on your device to provide the App's features, including transaction tracking, balances, budgets, savings goals, split-expense calculations, bill reminders, charts, reports, and personalised display preferences. The App uses your chosen reminder details to schedule local notifications.

5. Backups, exports and sharing

You can create backups and export information into files such as PDF, CSV and Excel spreadsheets. You choose whether to save or share those files and where to send them. When you use Android's file picker or share sheet, the selected destination or receiving app may process the file under its own privacy policy.

Important: The App’s database, backup files and exported reports are not encrypted by the App. Anyone who obtains an accessible copy of these files may be able to read the information they contain. Store and share them carefully.

The App may temporarily create export files for sharing and removes older temporary export files during later export operations. It also keeps a limited number of local safety-backup files used during restore operations.

6. App lock and biometrics

If you enable the optional PIN or recovery-code lock, the App stores salted, one-way hashes rather than the PIN or recovery code in plain text. The secret material is stored using Android secure storage backed by the Android Keystore.

If you enable biometric unlock, the biometric check is performed by Android through the device's authentication system. The App does not access or store your fingerprint, face scan or other biometric template. Biometric information remains under the control of your device's operating system and manufacturer.

The App lock is an additional access-control feature. It does not encrypt the App's SQLite database, backups or exported files.

7. Notifications and device permissions

Notifications are optional and are used for bill reminders you configure. Notifications are scheduled locally on your device; the App does not send reminder content to a notification server. You can deny notification permission or change it later in Android settings.

The Android app declares permissions for notifications, biometric authentication, vibration and receiving the device-restarted event so scheduled reminders can be restored. The App does not request these permissions to collect financial records from your device.

8. Data retention and deletion

Your records remain in the App's local storage until you delete them, clear the App's storage/data, or uninstall the App. You can also use the App's own features to remove or replace records where available. Files you have exported, saved or shared are outside the App's control and must be deleted separately from the locations or apps where you placed them.

Because the reviewed version does not maintain an app-operated account or server copy of your financial records, there is no remote account data for us to retrieve or delete.

9. Children's privacy

Namma Expense is not designed for or directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Third-party services

The App may use Android system features and other apps selected by you, such as the system file picker, biometric authentication, notification service and share sheet. Those Android services and receiving apps are governed by their respective providers' privacy terms. We do not control how a third-party app handles a file after you choose to share it with that app.

11. Security

We use the platform's secure storage for app-lock secrets and keep the App's financial records in local app storage. However, no device or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Protect your device with its own screen lock, keep your operating system updated, and take care when exporting or sharing financial files. The App does not encrypt its database, backup files or exported reports.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's features or applicable requirements change. The updated policy will be published at the same public policy address with a revised effective date. Please review it periodically.
